HOW WE DO IT

From the moment you walk through our door we truly do take care of you. Our exhaustive list of services ensures you will always find the right assistance, whatever that may be.

INITIAL ASSESSMENT

This is where we break down Fitness Goals and discuss Limitations such as Injuries and Medical Conditions. Afterwhich, we asses your Physical Condition, which includes Posture, Mobility, Strength, and Endurance.

PROGRAM DESIGN

Based on the initial assessment we tailor a detailed workout program that is customized to your goals. The program includes a warmup, power, strength, and endurance exercieses, with reps, sets, rest, tempo, duration, form ques, and cool down stretch routines.

NUTRITION COUNSELLING

Our nutrition approach is simple & straightforward. Our counselling includes Nutrition Guidelines, Tailored Recommendations, Nutrient Breakdown, Grocery List, and Meal Plan Examples.

BASIC TRAINING

Learning proper Form, Tempo, and ROM for executing the Basic Primal & Functional Movements. These include but are not limited to Basic Calisthenics & Lifting, Mobility, Stabilization, and Slow Steady State [SSS] Cardio.

SKILL TRAINING

Learning more Complex, Compound, Multi-planar, and Loaded Functional Movements. These include but are not limited to Loaded Calisthenics, Track & Field, Kettlebell, Powerlifting, MMA, Bodybuidling, and High Intensity Interval Training [HIIT] Cardio.

TECHNIQUE TRAINING

Learning Advanced and Highly Technical Movements. These include but are not limited to Strongman, Olympic Lifting, Intermediate & Advanced Calisthenics, and Gymnastics.

GROUP TRAINING

For those who value the energy and motivation, our small group sessions are between 6-10 athletes, with a maximum of 12 to ensure all athletes are attended to and are training safely and effectively.

PRIVATE TRAINING

For those who need a bit more attention, private training sessions consist of 3-5 athletes, each with their own tailored program.

PARTNER TRAINING

Find a partner and share a trainer. Each will have your own programs and workouts, yet we find similarities in your goals and perhaps share part the workout. Your trainer will in all cases be dedicated to only you and your partner during the session.

PERSONAL TRAINING

For those who need undivided attention, our one-on-one sessions have the highest results in our training department.
